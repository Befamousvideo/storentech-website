## 2. Services (site wording)
**AI Opportunity Map (the starting point).** Shows where time or revenue leaks, and which AI moves pay back first. You get a ranked map of where time and revenue hide, what to fix first, and what can wait.

**Build Plan.** After the map, the Build Plan is how we build what pays. Builds may include voice agents, website chat, operations automation, web apps, mobile apps, and custom-built solutions.

Front of house:
- **Speed-to-lead + after-hours closer.** Every web, call, and chat lead is touched fast, then booked or qualified.
- **Website chat + voice.** The same coverage on the number you publish and the site you already have.
- **Booking / appointment setting.** Holds on the real calendar, using the rules you already have for new and existing clients.

Revenue:
- **AI SDR / appointment-setter pod.** Outbound and inbound setting aimed at booked meetings on the calendar.
- **Sales follow-up.** Turns meeting notes into tasks, email, and CRM updates. Includes proposal nudges and pipeline hygiene so stalled deals don't go quiet.

Operations:
- **Fractional AI operations team.** Inbox triage, CRM hygiene, follow-up drafts, and a weekly ops report.
- **Owner's weekly command center.** A Monday brief covering cash, pipeline, stalled deals, reviews, ads, and five actions.
- **Recruiting / HR screening desk.** Role posts, inbound screening, scheduling, and scorecards. This covers screening and coordination, not legal advice.
- **Review + reputation engine.** Review asks, response drafts, and competitor watch.

Growth (available when the first build is paying):
- **AI content + distribution.** A planned set of 12–20 assets for the site, video, LinkedIn, and email, written to the offer.
- **Implementation studio.** We design the workflows, connect the tools, write the routines, train the owner, and stay on as operator.
- **White-label fulfillment.** Other agencies keep the client relationship while we build and run the grind behind their brand.

**Partner after the map.** Ongoing work is scoped from the evidence in your analysis: what to run, what to measure, and when to expand.
